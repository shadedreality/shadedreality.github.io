ChronoDeck privacy policy

ChronoDeck is a task planner for Android. Effective 29 October 2026.

In short: everything you put into ChronoDeck stays on your phone. ChronoDeck has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no internet access of its own. Nothing is collected or shared by ChronoDeck.

What ChronoDeck stores, and where

  • Your tasks, their history and your settings are saved in ChronoDeck's private storage on your phone.
  • Backups are files saved on your phone in Documents › ChronoDeck › Backups: one when you tap Backup records, and one each month that ChronoDeck saves by itself (it keeps the last 12 of those). They stay on your phone unless you move or share them yourself.
  • Home-screen widgets and reminders read the same data on your phone to show your tasks and notify you.

What leaves your phone

  • Nothing, by ChronoDeck itself. ChronoDeck doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't send data anywhere.
  • Update check (copies installed from Google Play): when ChronoDeck opens, at most once a day, and when you tap Check for Updates, it asks the Google Play app on your phone whether a newer version is available. Google Play handles that request under Google's privacy policy. ChronoDeck doesn't send your tasks or anything about you.
  • Feedback you choose to send: Settings › About › Feedback & Suggestions opens your own email app with your message and, at the end, the ChronoDeck version, Android version, phone model and how ChronoDeck was installed. You see all of it before you send, and you can change or remove it. It goes to chronodeckstrike@gmail.com and is used only to reply to you and improve ChronoDeck. Ask at that address and your emails will be deleted.
  • Your phone's own backup, if you've turned it on: Android can include ChronoDeck's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps, and put it back when you reinstall ChronoDeck or set up a new phone. Android and Google run that backup under Google's privacy policy; ChronoDeck can't see or change it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: task reminders, the morning summary and the day review.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time. You can turn this off in Android's settings; reminders may then come a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • View network connections: only to skip the update check when the phone is offline. It gives no internet access.
  • Storage, on Android 8 and 9 only: to save backup files in your Documents folder.

Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Clear records deletes your tasks and their history. Uninstalling ChronoDeck deletes everything in its private storage; backup files in Documents stay until you delete them in your Files app, and a copy in your phone's own backup is managed in your Google account settings.

Children

ChronoDeck isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
